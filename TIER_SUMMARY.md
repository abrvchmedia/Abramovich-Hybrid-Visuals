## Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Common Across All Tiers:
1. **Client Concentration Risk**
   - Mitigation: Focus on marketing retainers for recurring base
   - Diversify across industries

2. **Market Competition**
   - Mitigation: Premium positioning and high-quality delivery
   - Build strong portfolio and testimonials

3. **Talent Retention**
   - Mitigation: Competitive compensation (included in payroll)
   - Clear growth paths for team members

4. **Project Delays / Scope Creep**
   - Mitigation: Strong contracts and project management
   - Change order procedures

### Tier-Specific Risks:

**$100K Tier:**
- Single marketing client (100% of marketing revenue)
- Limited cash cushion
- Mitigation: Focus on client retention and immediate client acquisition

**$500K-$1M Tiers:**
- Scaling complexity
- Team building challenges
- Mitigation: Invest in operations and systems early

**$2M Tier:**
- Enterprise sales cycles
- Large team management
- Mitigation: Strong account management and delivery processes
